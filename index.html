ℹ️ About This Analytics Dashboard ▼
What is this tool?

This is a predictive dashboard that calculates whether an employee is likely to stay at the company or leave.

How does it work?

It is powered by a Machine Learning algorithm called a Decision Tree Classifier. Think of it like a smart flowchart that has learned from past company data to isolate critical turnover patterns.

What do you need to do?

Simply adjust the factors on the left—like Monthly Income, Age, and Overtime Status—using the sliders and dropdowns. When you click "Analyze", the algorithm processes your custom configuration and flags potential risks in real-time!
